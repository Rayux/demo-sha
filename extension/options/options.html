Settings

Subtitles and translations use your local Gemma model. Tutor explanations and questions use Groq (GPT OSS 120B) and require the API key below. Subtitle backup is optional and controlled separately.

Automatic chooses a working connection while your browser VPN is on or off. If the browser is blocked, Kage tries your local helper and remembers the working route briefly. Your key stays saved in Kage; the helper forwards it per request without saving it.

On Bilibili, YouTube, and Anime1.me, Kage starts downloading audio and transcribing when you open a video, so the first lines are ready before you press play. Only the tab you are viewing starts, and leaving the video stops work Kage started on its own. Turn off to prepare only when you choose Download audio & prepare subtitles.

Furigana is always generated locally, even with this option off. Leave off for faster Traditional Chinese subtitles. Upcoming lines are translated ahead when a matching timed track is available. Pause briefly to give preloading a head start.

YouTube media access (advanced)

Video preparation chooses its audio route automatically. Most YouTube playback sessions need no setting here. Optional media access is remembered by this browser and used automatically when available.
